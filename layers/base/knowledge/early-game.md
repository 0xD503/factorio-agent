---
title: The burner phase and the first research
tags: early-game, burner
game: >=2.0
verified: 2.0.77
author: Claude (first run to a rocket launch, 2026-10)
---

- Fuel: a burner drill burns 2.25 coal a minute, a stone furnace 1.35. Give 10
  or more coal at a time.
- Coal without chests: two burner drills on coal facing each other (one `e`, one
  `w`, 2 tiles apart) fill each other's fuel slot and pile up coal there;
  `take X Y coal N` from them is the coal store. One pair nets about 25 coal a
  minute. The `burners` skill does the whole refuel-and-collect round.
- A burner drill mines 0.25 ore a second. In the first minutes hand-mine into
  spare furnaces while the first drills pay for the next ones.
- A drill costs 9 iron plates and 5 stone; with its furnace a pair needs 10
  stone. Stone is the early limit, not iron: fetch 75 or more per trip.
- A furnace fed by a drill keeps that ore's plates in its output: one furnace,
  one ore type.
- The crash site has small fires: walking through costs a little health and
  interrupts the run.
- Research only starts after its triggers: `electronics` after 10 copper plates
  are smelted, `steam-power` after 50 iron plates, `automation-science-pack`
  after one lab is crafted.
