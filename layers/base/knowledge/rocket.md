- Rocket silo: 1000 concrete, 200 electric engines, 100 pipes, 200 processing
  units, 1000 steel; it can be hand-crafted (30 s).
- A rocket needs 100 rocket parts; each part takes 10 processing units, 10
  low-density structures and 10 rocket fuel. Four productivity-2 modules in the
  silo (+24%) cut that to about 81 crafts.
- A launch needs no satellite in 2.0 (`launch X Y`).
- Rocket fuel stacks only to 10: a chest holds little of it, so feed the silo in
  rounds or by belt.
- In the first run the long poles were, in order: purple and yellow science
  packs, then low-density structures (copper), then plastic (coal).
- Oil: two pumpjacks gave about 550k crude before bottoming out, roughly what
  one rocket needs. Find a third well early.
