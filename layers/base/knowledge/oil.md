title: Oil, plastic and the refinery's outputs
tags: oil, fluids

- Pumping the first crude triggers `oil-processing` research.
- An output a refinery cannot get rid of stops the whole refinery: a full
  solid-fuel chest at the end of the heavy-oil line stopped all plastic and
  sulfur. Give every output somewhere to go, and watch output chests.
- Plastic needs coal as well as gas (20 gas + 1 coal -> 2 plastic). Removing a
  coal tap can silently starve the plastic plants.
- Concrete is a fluid recipe in an assembling machine 2: 5 bricks + 1 iron ore +
  100 water -> 10 concrete.
- A wooden chest at a belt end holds 1,600 plates, but one inserter fills it at
  only 50 a minute.
